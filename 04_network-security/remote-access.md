# Remote Access

## Purpose

This document defines how remote access to systems and networks is securely provided and controlled. It ensures that external connectivity is authenticated, restricted, and monitored.

---

## Objectives

* Enable secure remote connectivity
* Protect systems from unauthorized external access
* Enforce strong authentication and access control
* Monitor and log remote activity

---

## Remote Access Approach

Remote access is provided through controlled and authenticated access paths.

Key principles:

* All remote access must be authenticated
* Access is restricted to authorized users
* Connectivity is limited to required systems
* Activity is logged and monitored

---

## Access Methods

* Secure remote access mechanisms are used for connectivity
* Direct exposure of internal systems is restricted
* Access is routed through controlled entry points

---

## Authentication

* Strong authentication is required for all remote access
* Multi-factor authentication is enforced for sensitive access
* Credentials are protected and monitored

---

## Access Control

* Access is granted based on role and necessity
* Privileged access is restricted and monitored
* Users are granted only the minimum access required
* Access is reviewed and updated regularly

---

## Network Restrictions

* Remote access is limited to specific network segments
* Communication paths are controlled and monitored
* Unnecessary access to internal systems is restricted

---

## Monitoring and Logging

* Remote access activity is logged
* Authentication attempts are tracked
* Suspicious access patterns are detected and investigated
* Logs are integrated with centralized monitoring systems

---

## Session Management

* Sessions are controlled and monitored
* Idle sessions are terminated
* Concurrent sessions may be restricted based on policy

---

## Risk Considerations

* Weak authentication increases risk of unauthorized access
* Overly broad access exposes internal systems
* Lack of monitoring reduces visibility
* Uncontrolled remote access increases attack surface

---

## Summary

Remote access must be securely controlled, authenticated, and monitored. Proper implementation reduces exposure to external threats and ensures that access to internal systems remains protected.
