# Endpoint Hardening

## Purpose

This document defines the approach for securing endpoints to reduce exposure to threats and unauthorized access.

---

## Objectives

* Protect endpoints from compromise
* Reduce attack surface
* Enforce secure configurations
* Maintain system integrity

---

## Hardening Approach

* Systems are configured using secure baselines
* Unnecessary services and applications are removed
* Default configurations are reviewed and adjusted
* Security controls are applied consistently

---

## Configuration Controls

* Strong authentication is enforced
* Administrative access is restricted
* Security settings are standardized
* System updates are applied regularly

---

## Access Control

* Access is based on role and necessity
* Privileged access is limited and monitored
* Local administrative rights are restricted

---

## Monitoring

* Endpoint activity is logged
* Suspicious behavior is detected
* Logs are integrated with centralized monitoring systems

---

## Risk Considerations

* Weak configurations increase exposure
* Excessive privileges lead to misuse
* Lack of updates leaves systems vulnerable

---

## Summary

Endpoint hardening reduces risk by securing system configurations and limiting exposure to threats.
