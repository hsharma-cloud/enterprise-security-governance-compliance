# Board-Level Security Summary

## Purpose

This document provides a high-level overview of the security posture, key risks, and overall effectiveness of security controls. It is intended for executive leadership and decision-makers.

---

## Security Overview

Security is implemented across identity, network, systems, data, and operations. Controls are designed to reduce risk, protect critical assets, and ensure reliable operations.

---

## Key Focus Areas

* Protection of critical systems and data
* Controlled access through identity and authentication
* Network segmentation and traffic control
* Continuous monitoring and detection
* Timely vulnerability remediation
* Data protection and encryption
* Business continuity and recovery

---

## Risk Overview

* High-risk items are identified and prioritized
* Risks are tracked and managed through defined processes
* Mitigation efforts are aligned with impact and likelihood
* Outstanding risks are monitored and reviewed

---

## Control Effectiveness

* Security controls are implemented across all domains
* Controls are validated and monitored regularly
* Metrics are used to measure performance
* Improvements are applied based on findings

---

## Incident and Operations Summary

* Security events are monitored continuously
* Detection capabilities identify suspicious activity
* Incident response processes manage and resolve issues
* Trends are analyzed to improve response effectiveness

---

## Data Protection

* Sensitive data is classified and protected
* Encryption is applied where required
* Access to data is controlled and monitored
* Data movement is tracked and restricted

---

## Resilience and Availability

* Critical systems are supported by continuity planning
* Backup and recovery processes are in place
* Recovery capabilities are tested and validated
* Operations are designed to minimize disruption
