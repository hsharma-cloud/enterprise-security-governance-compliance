# Risk Reporting

## Purpose

This document defines how security risks are communicated to stakeholders. It ensures that risks are clearly understood, tracked, and addressed.

---

## Objectives

* Provide visibility into security risks
* Support informed decision-making
* Track risk status and trends
* Ensure accountability for risk management

---

## Reporting Approach

Risk reporting provides a structured view of:

* Current risk levels
* High-risk issues
* Risk trends over time
* Status of mitigation efforts

---

## Risk Categories

| Category        | Description                                 |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **High Risk**   | Requires immediate attention and mitigation |
| **Medium Risk** | Requires planned mitigation                 |
| **Low Risk**    | Monitored with minimal urgency              |

---

## Key Risk Indicators

* Number of high-risk items
* Number of overdue risks
* Trends in risk levels
* Risk mitigation progress

---

## Reporting Content

Risk reports include:

* Summary of current risks
* Detailed view of high-risk items
* Status of mitigation actions
* Changes in risk levels over time

---

## Audience

| Audience          | Focus                             |
| ----------------- | --------------------------------- |
| **Management**    | Risk impact and priorities        |
| **Security Team** | Detailed risk analysis            |
| **System Owners** | Ownership and remediation actions |

---

## Frequency

* Risk reports are generated regularly
* High-risk issues are reported immediately
* Trends are reviewed periodically

---

## Communication

* Reports are clear and concise
* Technical details are simplified for stakeholders
* Critical risks are highlighted
* Actions and responsibilities are defined

---

## Integration with Risk Management

* Risk reporting is based on the risk register
* Updates reflect changes in risk status
* Metrics support risk visibility
* Reporting aligns with governance processes

---

## Risk Considerations

* Poor reporting reduces visibility
* Delayed reporting increases exposure
* Lack of clarity leads to poor decisions
* Missing data limits effectiveness

---

## Summary

Risk reporting ensures that security risks are visible, understood, and managed effectively. Clear communication supports informed decisions and strengthens overall security posture.
