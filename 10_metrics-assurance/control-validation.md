# Control Validation

## Purpose

This document defines the approach for validating the effectiveness of security controls across the environment. It ensures that controls are functioning as intended and providing the expected level of protection.

---

## Scope

This applies to all security controls, including identity, network, vulnerability management, security operations, and data protection.

---

## Objectives

- Verify that controls are implemented correctly
- Ensure controls are operating effectively
- Identify gaps or weaknesses
- Support continuous improvement

---

## Validation Approach

Control validation is performed through a combination of testing, monitoring, and review.

Key methods:

- Control testing
- Log and alert verification
- Configuration review
- Process validation

---

## Validation Methods

### Control Testing

- Simulate scenarios to verify control effectiveness
- Validate access restrictions and enforcement
- Confirm expected behavior under defined conditions

---

### Log Verification

- Confirm that events are logged correctly
- Validate that alerts are generated as expected
- Ensure logs are complete and accurate

---

### Configuration Review

- Review system and security configurations
- Ensure alignment with defined standards
- Identify misconfigurations or deviations

---

### Process Validation

- Verify that operational processes are followed
- Confirm that procedures produce expected outcomes
- Identify gaps in execution

---

## Control Areas

| Control Area | Validation Focus |
|-------------|----------------|
| Identity | Access control, authentication, privilege enforcement |
| Network | Segmentation, firewall rules, traffic control |
| Vulnerability Management | Scanning coverage, remediation effectiveness |
| Security Operations | Detection accuracy, response effectiveness |
| Data Protection | Encryption, access control, data monitoring |

---

## Frequency

- Critical controls are validated regularly
- High-risk areas are prioritized
- Validation activities are scheduled periodically
- Additional validation occurs after significant changes

---

## Reporting and Tracking

- Validation results are documented
- Issues are tracked and remediated
- Trends are analyzed over time
- Reports are shared with stakeholders

---

## Risk Considerations

- Unvalidated controls may fail silently
- Misconfigurations reduce effectiveness
- Lack of testing leads to false confidence
- Gaps in validation increase risk exposure
