# Security Strategy

## Purpose

This document defines the overall approach to securing systems, data, and operations across the environment. It establishes direction, priorities, and guiding principles for security.

---

## Objectives

- Protect critical systems and data
- Reduce risk through structured controls
- Ensure visibility across the environment
- Enable secure and reliable operations

---

## Strategic Principles

- Security decisions are driven by risk
- Identity is the primary control for access
- Access is restricted and continuously validated
- Monitoring is centralized and actionable
- Controls are regularly reviewed and improved

---

## Security Domains

Security is implemented across the following areas:

- Governance and risk management  
- Identity and access control  
- Network security and segmentation  
- Vulnerability management  
- Security operations and monitoring  
- Data protection and encryption  
- Resilience and recovery  

---

## Control Approach

- Controls are applied based on system criticality
- Access is granted based on role and necessity
- Security events are logged and monitored
- Vulnerabilities are identified and remediated

---

## Integration

Security is integrated across:

- Infrastructure and systems  
- Applications and data  
- Monitoring and response processes  

---

## Continuous Improvement

- Metrics are used to measure effectiveness
- Controls are validated regularly
- Improvements are implemented based on findings

---

## Summary

The security strategy provides a structured approach to protecting systems and data while enabling reliable operations. It aligns controls, monitoring, and processes to reduce risk and improve security effectiveness.
