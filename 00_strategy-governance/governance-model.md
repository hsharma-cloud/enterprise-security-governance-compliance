# Security Governance Model

## Purpose

This document defines the structure, roles, and responsibilities for managing security across the environment. It ensures accountability, clear ownership, and consistent decision-making.

---

## Objectives

- Establish clear ownership of security controls
- Define roles and responsibilities
- Ensure accountability for risk and security decisions
- Align security with operational priorities

---

## Governance Structure

Security governance is implemented through defined roles and coordinated responsibilities across teams.

---

## Roles and Responsibilities

| Role | Responsibility |
|-----|--------------|
| **Management** | Defines priorities, approves risk decisions, and provides oversight |
| **Security Team** | Defines controls, monitors security, and manages risk |
| **IT / Infrastructure** | Implements and maintains systems and controls |
| **System Owners** | Accountable for systems, data, and access |
| **Users** | Follow security policies and acceptable use |

---

## Decision-Making

- Security decisions are based on risk and impact
- High-risk issues are escalated to management
- Exceptions require formal approval
- Changes are reviewed and approved before implementation

---

## Control Ownership

- Every control must have a defined owner
- Owners are responsible for implementation and effectiveness
- Ownership is aligned with system or business responsibility

---

## Policy and Standards Alignment

- Policies define required security practices
- Standards provide implementation guidance
- Procedures define operational execution

---

## Oversight and Review

- Security posture is reviewed regularly
- Risks and issues are tracked and reported
- Governance processes are updated as needed

---

## Risk Considerations

- Lack of ownership leads to control gaps
- Unclear roles create accountability issues
- Poor governance increases risk exposure
- Inconsistent decision-making weakens security

---

## Summary

The governance model ensures that security is managed with clear ownership, accountability, and structured decision-making. It aligns security responsibilities across teams and supports effective risk management.
