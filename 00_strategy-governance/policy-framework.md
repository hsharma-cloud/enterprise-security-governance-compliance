# Policy Framework

## Purpose

This document defines the structure of security policies, standards, and procedures used to govern security practices across the environment.

---

## Objectives

- Establish consistent security requirements
- Define how controls are implemented and enforced
- Provide clear guidance for operational activities
- Ensure alignment across systems and teams

---

## Framework Structure

Security governance is structured into three levels:

| Level | Description |
|------|------------|
| **Policy** | High-level requirements and rules |
| **Standard** | Specific implementation requirements |
| **Procedure** | Step-by-step operational processes |

---

## Policies

Policies define mandatory requirements and expectations.

Examples:

- Access control requirements  
- Data protection requirements  
- Acceptable use guidelines  

---

## Standards

Standards define how policies are implemented.

Examples:

- Password and authentication standards  
- Encryption requirements  
- Network configuration standards  

---

## Procedures

Procedures define how tasks are performed.

Examples:

- User provisioning and deprovisioning  
- Incident response actions  
- Vulnerability remediation steps  

---

## Ownership and Maintenance

- Policies are defined and maintained by the security team
- Standards are maintained by technical teams
- Procedures are maintained by operational teams
- All documents are reviewed periodically

---

## Enforcement

- Policies are mandatory and must be followed
- Non-compliance is identified and addressed
- Exceptions require formal approval
- Violations are tracked and managed

---

## Review and Updates

- Documents are reviewed regularly
- Updates are made based on risk, changes, or findings
- Improvements are incorporated into the framework

---

## Risk Considerations

- Lack of structure leads to inconsistent controls
- Outdated policies reduce effectiveness
- Poor enforcement increases risk exposure
- Missing procedures impact operational execution

---

## Summary

The policy framework ensures that security requirements are clearly defined, consistently implemented, and effectively enforced. It provides structure and guidance for managing security across the environment.
