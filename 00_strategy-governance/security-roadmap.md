# Security Roadmap

## Purpose

This document outlines the planned improvements and future direction of security capabilities across the environment. It provides a structured approach to enhancing security over time.

---

## Objectives

- Strengthen security controls and coverage
- Address identified gaps and risks
- Improve visibility and detection capabilities
- Enhance operational efficiency

---

## Roadmap Approach

Security improvements are prioritized based on:

- Risk and impact
- Critical system requirements
- Operational needs
- Findings from metrics and validation

---

## Key Focus Areas

### Identity and Access

- Strengthen authentication mechanisms
- Improve access governance and reviews
- Enhance privileged access controls

---

### Network Security

- Improve segmentation and access control
- Refine firewall rules and monitoring
- Strengthen remote access controls

---

### Vulnerability Management

- Improve scan coverage and frequency
- Reduce remediation timelines
- Enhance tracking and reporting

---

### Security Operations

- Expand detection capabilities
- Improve alert accuracy and tuning
- Strengthen incident response processes

---

### Data Protection

- Increase encryption coverage
- Improve data monitoring and controls
- Enhance data loss prevention measures

---

### Resilience

- Improve backup coverage and validation
- Strengthen recovery processes
- Enhance continuity planning

---

## Implementation Phases

| Phase | Focus |
|------|------|
| **Phase 1** | Address high-risk gaps and critical systems |
| **Phase 2** | Improve control effectiveness and coverage |
| **Phase 3** | Optimize processes and enhance automation |

---

## Monitoring Progress

- Progress is tracked against defined goals
- Metrics are used to measure improvement
- Adjustments are made based on results

---

## Risk Considerations

- Delayed improvements increase exposure
- Lack of prioritization reduces effectiveness
- Untracked progress limits accountability
- Failure to adapt reduces long-term security posture

---

## Summary

The security roadmap provides a structured path for improving security capabilities over time. It ensures that enhancements are aligned with risk, operational needs, and long-term objectives.
