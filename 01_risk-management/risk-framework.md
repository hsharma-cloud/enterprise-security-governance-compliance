# Risk Management Framework

## Purpose

This document defines the approach for identifying, assessing, and managing risk across the environment. It ensures that security decisions are aligned with business impact and operational priorities.

---

## Objectives

- Identify and evaluate risks across systems and processes
- Prioritize risks based on impact and likelihood
- Support informed decision-making
- Reduce overall risk exposure

---

## Risk Management Approach

Risk management is a continuous process that includes:

- Risk identification  
- Risk assessment  
- Risk treatment  
- Risk monitoring  

---

## Risk Identification

Risks are identified through:

- Asset classification  
- Vulnerability assessments  
- Security monitoring and incidents  
- Configuration and control reviews  

---

## Risk Assessment

Risks are evaluated based on:

| Factor | Description |
|-------|------------|
| **Impact** | Potential effect on systems, data, or operations |
| **Likelihood** | Probability of occurrence |

Risk levels are determined using a combination of impact and likelihood.

---

## Risk Levels

| Level | Description |
|------|------------|
| **High** | Significant impact requiring immediate attention |
| **Medium** | Moderate impact requiring timely mitigation |
| **Low** | Limited impact with minimal urgency |

---

## Risk Treatment Options

| Treatment | Description |
|----------|------------|
| **Mitigate** | Apply controls to reduce risk |
| **Accept** | Acknowledge and monitor risk |
| **Transfer** | Shift risk through external means |
| **Avoid** | Eliminate risk by removing exposure |

---

## Risk Ownership

- Each risk must have a defined owner  
- Owners are responsible for mitigation and tracking  
- High-risk items require management visibility  

---

## Risk Monitoring

- Risks are tracked through a centralized register  
- Status is reviewed regularly  
- Changes in environment trigger reassessment  
- Metrics are used to measure risk trends  

---

## Integration with Security Controls

- Risk drives prioritization of controls  
- Vulnerability management aligns with risk levels  
- Monitoring and detection focus on high-risk areas  
- Identity and access controls reduce exposure  

---

## Risk Considerations

- Unidentified risks increase exposure  
- Poor prioritization delays mitigation  
- Lack of ownership leads to unmanaged risk  
- Infrequent review reduces effectiveness  

---

## Summary

The risk management framework provides a structured approach to identifying and managing risk. It ensures that security efforts are aligned with impact and supports effective decision-making across the environment.
