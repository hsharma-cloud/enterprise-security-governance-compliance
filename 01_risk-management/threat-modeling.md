# Threat Modeling

## Purpose

This document defines the approach for identifying and analyzing potential threats to systems, applications, and data. It helps anticipate attack scenarios and improve security controls.

---

## Objectives

- Identify potential threats to systems and data
- Understand how systems may be attacked
- Prioritize controls based on risk
- Improve security design and monitoring

---

## Threat Modeling Approach

Threat modeling focuses on understanding:

- What needs to be protected  
- Who or what may pose a threat  
- How systems can be attacked  
- What controls can reduce risk  

---

## Key Components

### Assets

- Identity systems  
- Applications and servers  
- Databases and storage  
- Network infrastructure  

---

### Threat Sources

- External attackers  
- Insider misuse  
- Malware and automated attacks  
- Misconfigurations and human error  

---

### Attack Vectors

- Credential compromise  
- Network exploitation  
- Application vulnerabilities  
- Privilege escalation  

---

## Analysis Process

1. Identify critical assets  
2. Identify potential threats  
3. Determine possible attack paths  
4. Assess impact and likelihood  
5. Define mitigating controls  

---

## Common Threat Scenarios

| Scenario | Description |
|---------|------------|
| Credential Theft | Unauthorized access using stolen credentials |
| Privilege Escalation | Gaining higher-level access |
| Lateral Movement | Moving between systems within the network |
| Data Exfiltration | Unauthorized data transfer |
| Service Disruption | Impacting availability of systems |

---

## Mitigation Strategies

- Strong authentication and access control  
- Network segmentation  
- Continuous monitoring and detection  
- Vulnerability management and patching  

---

## Integration with Security Controls

- Identity controls prevent unauthorized access  
- Network controls limit movement  
- Monitoring detects suspicious activity  
- Risk management prioritizes mitigation  

---

## Risk Considerations

- Unidentified threats increase exposure  
- Weak controls allow exploitation  
- Lack of visibility delays detection  
- Poor analysis leads to ineffective mitigation  

---

## Summary

Threat modeling provides a structured way to understand potential attack scenarios and improve security controls. It strengthens the ability to prevent, detect, and respond to threats.
