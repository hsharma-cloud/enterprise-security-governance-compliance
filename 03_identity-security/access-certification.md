# Access Certification

## Purpose

This document defines the process for reviewing and validating user access to systems and data. It ensures that access rights remain appropriate and aligned with business needs.

---

## Scope

This applies to all user accounts, privileged accounts, and service accounts across systems and applications.

---

## Objectives

- Ensure users have appropriate access
- Remove unnecessary or excessive permissions
- Identify inactive or orphaned accounts
- Maintain accountability for access decisions

---

## Certification Process

### 1. Access Review Initiation

- Access reviews are conducted periodically
- Scope includes critical and high-value systems
- Reviews are initiated by the security or governance team

---

### 2. Review by Asset Owners

- Asset owners review user access
- Access is validated against job function and responsibilities
- Unnecessary access is identified

---

### 3. Remediation

- Excess access is removed
- Inactive accounts are disabled or deleted
- Privileged access is reviewed and restricted if needed

---

### 4. Approval and Documentation

- All decisions are documented
- Changes are approved and recorded
- Evidence is retained for audit purposes

---

## Review Frequency

| System Type | Frequency |
|------------|----------|
| Critical Systems | Quarterly |
| High-Risk Systems | Quarterly |
| Standard Systems | Semi-Annually |

---

## Key Controls

- Access must be reviewed by the appropriate owner
- Privileged access is reviewed more frequently
- Orphaned accounts must be removed
- Certification results must be documented

---

## Risk Considerations

- Excess access increases risk of misuse
- Inactive accounts may be exploited
- Lack of review leads to privilege accumulation
- Untracked access reduces accountability

## Summary

Access certification ensures that access rights remain appropriate over time. Regular review and validation reduce the risk of unauthorized access and strengthen overall access control.
